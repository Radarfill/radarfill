# Code of Conduct

RadarFill is a small academic team, and we want it to be a respectful place to work and learn.

## We commit to
- Being kind and constructive in reviews and discussions. Critique the work, not the person.
- Giving credit honestly: co-authorship, ideas, code and data sources.
- Research integrity: no fabricated or cherry-picked results, and no peeking at the test fold.
- Welcoming questions, especially from newcomers to the project.

## Not acceptable
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting
Contact the project maintainers privately through the organization's owners. If a concern involves a maintainer, contact the project supervisor at the University of Moratuwa. Reports are handled confidentially.
